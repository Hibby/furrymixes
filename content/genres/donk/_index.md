---
title: "Donk"
description: "Thumpy loud music"
---

Donk, often also called Scouse House or Bounce House is one of the greatest modern genres. A distillation of all the best forms of hard house into one fast, loud and thumpy style with a bassline that can only be described as "a metal pipe being smashed with a sandal". 

The enlightened fans of donk come together to bounce and wiggle to its fast and primal beats, and often enjoy modern interpretations where mashups, covers, comedic drops and diversions are dropped in to make the set more entertaining.

Artists at the forefront of this genre include but are not limited to The Blackout Crew, known most for [put a donk on it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ckMvj1piK58) and [put a donk on it 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=COc_r9BS9aQ)
