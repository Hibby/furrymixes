With having so many gigs this summer (a wonderful problem to have) and doing a bunch of travel around them, there was no way I could put together something up to my own standards which I'd have been proud of for all of them.

This is the first set that I've "toured" with minor variations, playing it out at Kiez Burn, Animalz Folsom Berlin, BFC & Liminal Folk. It got refined and rejigged with each performance, tweaked to work for that particular crowd, but this is the definitive version of it!

Huge love to every single person who welcomed me onto their stage, and for everyone who threw down to this one across the three countries I played it out! More to come soon! <3~
