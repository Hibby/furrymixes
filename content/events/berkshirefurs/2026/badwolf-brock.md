Playing live at the BerkshireFurs Last Howl of Summer Party 2026, my Opening set! This party marked the end of summer and the last howl of a terrific season, and so I chose to open with some bright, summery Disco - both classic and modern. And, of course, to include a tribute to the late, great Dolly Parton, whose music lifted us all up.
