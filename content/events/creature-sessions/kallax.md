Was privileged to perform the opening set at the August edition of Creature Sessions @ Basement 45 in Bristol

I wanted to go for a disco-y funky vibe this time, something to warm the crowd up having come over from the Bristol Furs meet. I'm quite happy with how it played on the night! 

A big shoutout to Liamm (www.mixcloud.com/dj-byksr) for suggesting the opening song to me. It was the inspiration I needed and set the tone for the rest of the set perfectly ❤️

This was recorded the morning before, and is almost exactly as it played out on the night!
