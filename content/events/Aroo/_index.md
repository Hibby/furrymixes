### *aroo* is a platform for live music, expression, and community.

we want to build a space where marginalized people can celebrate their artistry and play loud, weird music. if you are queer, if you are a person of color, if you are poor, if you are disabled, if you are hurting, we want you to feel safe to party with us. building community in the midst of social hostility is a necessary radical act of love and compassion.
