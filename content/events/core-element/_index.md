Core Element is an immersive fantasy, music and costuming festival, hosted
within the enchanting grounds of Elvaston castle show grounds and forest.
Venture into the forest where two spectacular stages await your discovery, alive
with incredible acts and talented DJ's.  As the sun goes down, the forest
awakens, transforming into a mesmerising, technicolour display of light and
decoration.
